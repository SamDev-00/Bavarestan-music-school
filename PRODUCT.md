# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Parents choosing a serious first music school for their children, and adult beginners in Tehran who want structured lessons without intimidation. They arrive uncertain about level, schedule, and whether the place feels credible. Success means booking a free introduction or submitting the enrollment form with confidence.

## Product Purpose

A marketing site for آموزشگاه موسیقی باورستان that communicates a modern conservatory: crisp teaching standards, clear courses, and a calm path from first lesson to stage. Primary conversion is consultation / new-term registration.

## Brand Personality

Crisp. Structured. Assured.

Feels like a daylight practice room with a grand piano and a clear syllabus — not a kids’ playroom, not a nightlife promo, not a tech startup.

## Anti-references

- Colorful children’s academy templates (cartoon icons, rainbow pills)
- Flashy Instagram music-promo aesthetics (neon, glow, sticker overlays)
- Generic SaaS / startup landing pages (purple gradients, metric strips, identical icon cards)

## Design Principles

1. **Conservatory clarity** — Structure and hierarchy do the persuading; decoration stays quiet.
2. **Credibility before charm** — Parents and beginners should feel safe, not sold to.
3. **One job per section** — Each fold answers one question: what, who, why, or how to join.
4. **Brand as place** — Imagery and typography suggest a real teaching room, not an abstract music app.
5. **Accessible by default** — Strong contrast, RTL-native Persian type, reduced-motion respect.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast. Support RTL Persian layout throughout. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Form labels stay visible; focus states are clear. Avoid relying on color alone for meaning.
